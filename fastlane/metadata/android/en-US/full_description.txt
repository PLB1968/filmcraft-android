A mobile video editor in the spirit of Premiere Rush, open source, with a Rust engine and a Kotlin UI.

Rushcraft is a video editor for Android, built on the FilmCraft engine. It brings the desktop editor's layout and tools to your phone: Source and Program monitors, tracks, the Razor, Ripple and Rolling edit tools, and everything stays on your phone: no account, no cloud.

Edit
* Start from your photo library, from files or blank; open videos shared from other apps
* Trim, move, split, delete, duplicate and replace clips; undo and redo
* Speed from 0.1x to 10x (the sound keeps its pitch), freeze frames, volume, fades, extract audio
* Record a voiceover

Look
* 31 filters and 23 effects, opacity, mirror and flip; move, scale and turn clips on the preview
* 50 transitions, intros and outros
* Text with presets, the phone's fonts, colours and layout
* 11 aspect ratios and blank clips

Captions and speech
* Captions made on the phone by a speech model, then edited, styled and burned into the video
* Text to speech with two built-in voices and nine natural voices
* The speech models are downloaded once, only when you ask; the sound never leaves the phone

Export
* 720p, 1080p or 4K at 24 to 60 fps, saved to your Movies folder and ready to share

Works upright and sideways. Rushcraft is in beta.
